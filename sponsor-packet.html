Who we are

Small team.
Real work.

At River Valley High School, students design, fabricate, wire, program, and test a combat robot. Most start with no experience. They learn by doing — and by fixing what breaks.

Sponsors make that possible. Your support covers the parts, tools, fees, and travel that turn an after-school club into a serious learning experience.
