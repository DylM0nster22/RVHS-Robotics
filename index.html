What we do

The whole build.

Students handle the CAD, the 3D printing, the wiring, and the driving. We learn the tools as we need them, then fix what breaks.

This is our second year as a club. The robot is small, the shop is one room, and the chassis and armor were designed and printed by students.

About the club

Design

Model the chassis, weapon, and armor in CAD, then print a version you can hold.

Build

Solder the electronics, assemble the drive, and test-fit the parts until they stop fighting each other.

Compete

Practice driving, make repairs, and take the robot to competition. Dates go on Instagram.
