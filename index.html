Who we are

More than
a robot.

River Valley Robotics is the combat robotics club at River Valley High School. In our second year, students take an idea from a sketch to a machine that can take a hit — designing the frame, printing parts, wiring electronics, and learning to fix what breaks.

We're here for the students who like to know how things work, and for the ones who are still figuring that out. No prior experience needed. What matters is showing up, asking questions, and being willing to try again.

What we do

Every part of the build.

A combat robot is a team project. Students choose a subteam, learn the tools, and own a real piece of the machine — or help tell its story.

01

Mechanical

Build a frame, armor, and weapon mounts that can take a hit.

  • CAD & prototyping
  • Drills, saws, 3D printers
  • Armor & assembly
02

Electrical

Power, wiring, and motor control that keep the robot moving.

  • Motors & speed controllers
  • Battery & power
  • Wiring & troubleshooting
03

Software & drive

Make the machine move exactly how the driver expects, every match.

  • Robot controls
  • Match strategy
  • Testing & practice
04

Business & outreach

Keep the team funded, organized, and connected to the community.

  • Sponsorships & grants
  • Social media & media
  • Events & STEM outreach

How a season works

From idea
to the arena.

The calendar changes every year, but the rhythm is the same: learn the rules, design hard, build fast, test often, and compete with everything you've got.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Learn the rules

Read the competition rules, study other bots, and decide what the team can build and drive well.

02 / DESIGN

Sketch & prototype

Trade ideas, build quick mock-ups, and choose a design the whole team can execute.

03 / BUILD

Make it real

CAD, cut, drill, print, wire, and code. Subteams work in parallel and solve problems together.

04 / TEST

Break it, fix it

Drive practice, stress tests, and late-night debugging. The robot gets better every run.

05 / COMPETE

Take the arena

Pass safety, make weight, and compete. Then come home and make the next one better.
