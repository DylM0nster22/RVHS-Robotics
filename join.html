Questions

Before you start.

Still unsure? That's normal. Come by and see what it's like.

Do I need experience?

No. We teach the tools, the software, and the safety rules. Curiosity and showing up matter more than experience.

Can I join in the middle of the year?

Yes. The start of a season is the easiest time to onboard, but we can help you catch up and find a job that fits where the team is.

What if I can only come once a week?

Come once a week. Consistency matters more than hours, especially at the start. Build season gets busier, but there is work for every schedule.

Do I need to buy my own tools?

No. The team provides tools and safety equipment. If you have your own safety glasses or hand tools, bring them, but nothing is required.
