Mentor info

For students

Start where
you are.

You do not need to know how to use a drill, write code, or design a part. You need to be curious, follow safety rules, and be part of the team.

New members rotate through the shop, learn the basics, and then choose a subteam. Older students and mentors teach the tools. The work is real, and it goes into a combat robot that competes.

What you'll get

  • Hands-on experience with tools, CAD, electronics, and code
  • A team that respects beginners and rewards reliability
  • Something real for your resume, college applications, and portfolio
  • Match days, a pit crew, and a group that becomes a second family

For parents & mentors

You don't have
to be an expert.

Some of the most useful help has nothing to do with robots: driving students to events, organizing a parts bin, bringing dinner to a build night, or asking the question that makes a student explain their idea more clearly.

If you do have technical experience — machining, electrical, software, CAD, project management — we would love to put it to work. Mentors teach by asking questions, not by taking the controller.

Questions

Before you start.

Still unsure? That's normal. Come by and see what it's like.

What if I can only come once a week?

Come once a week. Consistency matters more than hours, especially at the start. Build season gets busier, but there is work for every schedule.

Do I need to buy my own tools?

No. The team provides tools and safety equipment. If you have your own safety glasses or hand tools, bring them, but nothing is required.

Can I join in the middle of the year?

Yes, though the start of a season is the easiest time to onboard. We'll help you catch up and find a job that fits where the team is.

Is the team competitive?

We compete, and we want to do well. But the bigger goal is learning. Students who are new to robotics are not a liability — they are the point.

What do parents actually do?

Transportation, food, event help, fundraising, supervision, and professional advice. Every family has different capacity; whatever you can offer is useful.

Is there a fee?

Ask us about current team fees and materials costs. We work to keep cost from being a barrier and can talk through options confidentially.
