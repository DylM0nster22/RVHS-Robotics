About us

A team, not just a machine.

River Valley Robotics is a student-led combat robotics club at River Valley High School. We build robots, but the real project is the people doing the building.

Our story

We learn
by building.

River Valley Robotics is in its second year as a club robotics team. Every season starts with a question: can we build a machine that survives the arena? Then a room full of students tries to answer it.

Some of us came in knowing how to solder. Most of us didn't. What we share is a willingness to learn in public: to sketch an idea, ask for help, cut the wrong piece, and try again. That is what combat robotics looks like up close.

Along the way, students practice CAD, fabrication, electronics, programming, project management, and communication. Those skills travel. So does the habit of solving a problem with a team.

What we value

How we work.

Four rules that hold up whether we're drilling a frame at lunch or packing the pit at a competition.

01

Learn by doing

The fastest way to understand something is to build it, break it, and try again.

02

Everyone contributes

No benchwarmers. Every member owns a job and a real piece of the build.

03

Build it safe

Safety glasses on, tools respected, checklists followed — every time, for everyone.

04

Give back

We share what we learn with our school and our community, not just the scoreboard.

Mentors

The adults
behind the build.

Our mentors are teachers, parents, alumni, and local professionals who give time, tools, and perspective. They don't build the robot for us. They help us learn how to build it ourselves.

You don't need to be a robotics expert to help. We need people who can teach a skill, supervise a shop, drive students, organize a fundraiser, or simply show up consistently.

Questions

Good to know.

Still curious? Email us at rvrobotics@ycusd.org or come find us in Room 408.

What is combat robotics?

Combat robotics is exactly what it sounds like: students design and build remote-controlled robots that compete in matches. The goal is to disable or outlast the opponent while staying inside the competition's safety and build rules.

Do I need robotics experience to join?

No. Most of our members start with no robotics background. We teach the tools, the software, and the safety rules from the beginning. Curiosity and reliability matter more than experience.

Who can join the team?

River Valley High School students in grades 9 through 12. We also welcome parents, alumni, and community members as mentors and volunteers.

When do you meet?

Thursdays & Fridays in Room 408. Check Google Classroom (code gftqhxdh) or the team Instagram for schedule changes, especially around competitions.

What does it cost?

Ask us about current team fees and materials costs. We work hard to keep cost from being a barrier and can talk through options with families.

How can my business help?

Sponsorships of any size help with parts, tools, event fees, and travel. In-kind donations of materials, machining time, or professional expertise are welcome too. Start on the sponsorship page.
