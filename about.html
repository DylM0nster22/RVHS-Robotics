The short version

We started
in fall 2025.

The club began with a first meeting in September 2025 and a simple goal: build a combat robot and learn what it takes to keep it running.

This year we're doing it again with a new robot. Students design parts in CAD, print and assemble them, wire the electronics, and test the robot until it does what we want. On a small team, everyone ends up helping with more than one job.

We meet Thursdays and Fridays in Room 408. Announcements go out through Google Classroom (code gftqhxdh) and @rv.robotics.

What we do

Four kinds of work.
One robot.

Pick a part of the build that sounds interesting. You can try a few things before you settle on one.

Design

CAD & parts

Model the chassis, weapon, and armor. Print prototypes and fix what doesn't fit.

Build

Shop & assembly

Drill, tap, print, and assemble. Learn the tools and keep the robot in one piece.

Wiring

Electronics & code

Solder the speed controllers, route the wiring, and get the motors moving in the right direction.

Drive

Testing & repairs

Practice driving, find the weak points, and fix them before the next match.

Questions

Good to know.

Still curious? Email rvrobotics@ycusd.org or come find us in Room 408.

What is combat robotics?

Combat robotics is a category of robot competition where students build small, remote-controlled machines that compete in matches. The robots have to follow a set of safety and build rules, and the goal is to disable or outlast the other robot.

Do I need robotics experience to join?

No. Most of the skills are learned in the shop. We teach the tools, the software, and the safety rules from the beginning. Curiosity and showing up matter more than experience.

Who can join?

River Valley High School students in grades 9 through 12. Parents, alumni, and community members can help as mentors and volunteers.

When do you meet?

Thursdays & Fridays in Room 408. Check Google Classroom (code gftqhxdh) or the team Instagram for schedule changes, especially around competitions.

Does it cost anything?

Ask us about current team fees and materials costs. We don't want cost to be the reason someone doesn't join, and we can talk through options with families.
