two minutes.

Why sponsor

A small team
with a real budget.

A combat robot is not free. Motors, speed controllers, batteries, filament, tools, and entry fees add up fast. Your support lets students build a robot, test it, break it, and fix it.

We're a second-year club, and the first names are already on the donor wall. Local businesses and families can help us get the parts we need. You don't need to be a robotics company.

Sponsor questions

The details.

Need something that isn't here? Email or call us and we'll give you a straight answer.

How do I donate online?

Use the form at the top of this page, or open hcb.hackclub.com/donations/start/rvhs-robotics-club in a new tab. You can give any amount, one time or monthly. Hack Club Bank hosts the form, the receipts, and the public ledger.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Online gifts are tax-deductible through Hack Club Bank, a 501(c)(3). You'll get an emailed receipt as soon as the gift clears, so keep it for your records. For checks, transfers, or in-kind support, talk to us first so we route the gift through the right account.

Can we donate materials or services instead of cash?

Yes. Aluminum, fasteners, filaments, tools, machining time, printing, food for a build night, and professional expertise are all useful. We'll work with you on an in-kind acknowledgement.

What does the money go toward?

Parts for the robot, shop supplies, tools that wear out, event entry fees, and travel. If you want your support to go toward something specific, tell us and we'll confirm it in writing.

How does the donor wall work?

The donor wall reads our public Hack Club Bank ledger, so a new public gift appears there without anyone editing the site. Your name and level come from the donation. Levels follow total giving: Friend under $100, then Supporter at $100 (that level includes a logo on this page), Partner, Builder, and Champion. Anonymous gifts are listed as Anonymous. Send us a logo and we'll swap it in for your initials.

When is the deadline for logo placement?

Shirt and robot deadlines are set each season. Sponsor early to guarantee placement on both. Website and social recognition can be added year-round.
