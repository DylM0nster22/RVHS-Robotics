Why it matters

Your business.
Their launchpad.

A combat robotics team is a small company run by students. They plan budgets, manage deadlines, solve technical problems, and explain their work to adults. Now in our second season, sponsorship is what makes that experience possible.

Every dollar goes into the build: parts, tools, event fees, travel, and the supplies that let a student try an idea a second time. Your name goes where the work happens — on the robot, the shirts, the website, and the pit.

Sponsor questions

The details.

Need something that isn't here? Call or email us — we'll find a straight answer.

Is my sponsorship tax deductible?

Ask us for the current tax information before you give. Donations may be processed through the RVHS Booster Club or the district, depending on the type of support. We'll give you the correct receipt and documentation.

Can we donate materials or services instead of cash?

Yes. Aluminum, fasteners, tools, machining time, printing, food for build nights, and professional expertise are all valuable. We'll work with you on an in-kind acknowledgement.

Can we choose what our sponsorship supports?

Usually. We can direct support toward a specific need such as registration, a subsystem, safety equipment, or travel. We'll confirm the details in writing.

How will our logo be used?

With your approval, on the team website, shirts, robot, event banner, and social posts according to your level. We'll ask for a vector logo and brand guidelines when you commit.

When is the deadline for logo placement?

Shirt and robot deadlines are set each season. Sponsor early to guarantee placement on both. Website and social recognition can be added year-round.

Will the team visit our business?

Champion sponsors can schedule a robot demo or a student presentation. We can also host your team at the school shop, subject to district visitor rules.
