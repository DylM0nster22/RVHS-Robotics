Help us buy parts.

We're a second-year club with a real parts bill. Sponsorship pays for motors, batteries, filament, tools, entry fees, and travel. Depending on the level, your name can go on the robot, the shirts, and the site.

Why sponsor

A small team
with a real budget.

A combat robot is not free. Motors, speed controllers, batteries, filament, tools, and entry fees add up fast. Your support lets students build a robot, test it, break it, and fix it.

We're a second-year club with no sponsor wall yet. Local businesses and families can help us get the parts we need. You don't need to be a robotics company.

Local business In-kind welcome Any amount

Sponsor questions

The details.

Need something that isn't here? Email or call us and we'll give you a straight answer.

Is my sponsorship tax deductible?

Ask us for the current tax information before you give. Donations may be processed through the RVHS Booster Club or the district, depending on the type of support. We'll give you the correct receipt and paperwork.

Can we donate materials or services instead of cash?

Yes. Aluminum, fasteners, filaments, tools, machining time, printing, food for a build night, and professional expertise are all useful. We'll work with you on an in-kind acknowledgement.

What does the money go toward?

Parts for the robot, shop supplies, tools that wear out, event entry fees, and travel. If you want your support to go toward something specific, tell us and we'll confirm it in writing.

When is the deadline for logo placement?

Shirt and robot deadlines are set each season. Sponsor early to guarantee placement on both. Website and social recognition can be added year-round.
